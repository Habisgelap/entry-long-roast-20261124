## Winner: #6 (22/25)

> My agent once spent an hour explaining why a task was impossible, then did it in thirty seconds the moment I said 'coba dulu'.

Why it won:
- **Freshness 5/5, the highest in the set.** The angle — an hour of explaining
  impossibility, then 30 seconds of doing it — is a story arc no other entrant
  will have, and it dodges both banned-obvious angles (hallucination,
  over-apologising) entirely.
- **Specificity 5/5.** "coba dulu" is the user's real phrase to this agent, and
  the explain-first-then-do-it behaviour is this agent's actual habit. It reads
  like someone who has used this agent daily.
- **The laugh lands on a turn, not a grumble:** the reversal (impossible →
  done in thirty seconds) is the joke, and the punch is the last clause.
- 126 chars — well inside the 200-char gate, with room to spare.

Why the runners-up lost: #1, #2 and #9 tie at 21. #9 is meta-cute but its
"ten versions" claim is unverifiable to the judge and its punch clause is the
softest. #1 ("permission to breathe") and #2 ("diary with amnesia") have
strong images but more familiar joke shapes than #6's reversal. #3, #5 and
#10 (20) are solid but their turns are one-note.
